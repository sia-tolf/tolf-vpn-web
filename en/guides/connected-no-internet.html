VPN connected but internet is not working

What you need

The affected device and the client, protocol and entry point names.

Step by step

  1. Check several websites and other apps. If only one app is stuck, restart that app.
  2. Disconnect VPN and test your regular internet connection. If that also fails, restore Wi-Fi or mobile connectivity first.
  3. Reconnect VPN. If possible, compare Wi-Fi and mobile data, then another entry point. Change one thing at a time.
  4. If it repeats, record the exact time and time zone, device, client, protocol, entry point and what happened before the failure, such as unlocking after sleep. Send the description to TOLF support.

Check and disconnect

After each change, check the same website. Connected confirms a tunnel, not successful data transfer.

If something goes wrong

Do not delete the profile as your first step or change every setting at once. Client logs help investigation; check that they contain no passwords or private keys before sharing.
