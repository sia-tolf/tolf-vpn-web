VPN billed by the gigabyte

With TOLF you buy traffic, not connection days. Your paid balance does not expire at the end of the month.

Open TOLF VPN

How traffic is counted

VPN downloads and uploads on devices linked to your account are counted. Trial and paid balances are displayed separately in your account. 1 GB means 1 billion bytes.

No monthly subscription

Paid traffic has no expiry date. Top-ups add to your remaining balance. There is no subscription or automatic monthly renewal. Available payment methods and the final amount are shown in your account before purchase.

A 1 GB trial

A 1 GB trial is available once per registered account to test the connection, without a bank card or time limit. Once claimed, your account shows the remaining trial balance.

VPN for iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows

The connection method depends on the device. TOLF provides IKEv2 and AnyConnect settings. On iPhone and iPad you can also use Shadowrocket with an OpenConnect connection. Shadowrocket is purchased separately and does not include TOLF traffic.

Moscow, Riga and New York

TOLF has entry points in Moscow, Riga and New York. Entry point and connection method availability are checked during setup. Speed depends on your device, provider and route; a fixed speed is not guaranteed.

Getting started

Open TOLF VPN and sign in or create an account. Choose your device and protocol, then use quick or manual setup. Purchase options and your balance are available under Traffic and payments.
